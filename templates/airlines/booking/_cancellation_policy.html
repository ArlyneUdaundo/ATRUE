Flight Cancellation & Refund Policy

Flight cancellation and refund eligibility depend on the airline’s fare rules, the reason for cancellation, the timing of the request, and any applicable insurance policy. Insurance claims are assessed separately under the purchased policy.

  1. 1

    Submit a cancellation request

    Open the booking, choose “Request Cancellation”, and give the booking reference and the reason for cancelling.

  2. 2

    Provide supporting documents

    For illness, a medical or family emergency, or another potentially eligible reason, upload the documents that reason needs. Only documents relevant to the policy in force are requested.

  3. 3

    Verify eligibility

    The fare conditions, the airline’s cancellation rules, the deadline for the request and any insurance cover are checked against the booking.

  4. 4

    Review the request

    A booking administrator or the airline reviews the request and the documents. A request is never approved automatically just because a reason was entered.

  5. 5

    Determine the refund

    The outcome is one of: a full refund, a partial refund after applicable fees, a refund under the purchased insurance subject to its terms, or no refund under the applicable conditions.

  6. 6

    Process the approved refund

    For approved requests the amount, payment method, status and processing date are recorded, and you are notified when the refund is processed.

Fare rules, changes and deadlines

Submit cancellation and rebooking requests as early as possible and before the departure deadline stated in the applicable fare conditions. Any cancellation or change fee is determined by those fare conditions. Non-refundable fares may not return the fare amount; refundable taxes or other amounts depend on the airline’s rules.

No-show and airline-initiated cancellations

If a passenger does not arrive for check-in or boarding, the airline’s no-show conditions apply and onward or return segments may be affected. If the airline cancels or materially changes a flight, contact the booking team to review the options offered by the airline, which may include rebooking or a refund under its rules.

Refund processing and insurance

Approved refunds are returned through the applicable payment process. Processing time depends on the airline, payment provider and bank. Insurance claims are submitted to and assessed by the insurer under the policy terms; purchasing insurance does not guarantee an airline refund.
