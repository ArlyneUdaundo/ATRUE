Travel insurance does not automatically guarantee a refund. A claim must meet the purchased policy’s coverage conditions and exclusions. Airline refunds and insurance claims are handled separately. A request is never approved automatically just because a reason was entered.

Step-by-step process

  1. The passenger submits a cancellation request from their booking.
  2. The passenger provides the reason for cancellation and relevant supporting documents.
  3. The system checks the applicable airline cancellation rules and insurance coverage.
  4. An authorized administrator, airline representative, or insurance claims handler reviews the request.
  5. The passenger receives an eligibility decision and an explanation of any applicable refund or fees.
  6. Approved refunds or insurance claims proceed through the appropriate payment or claims process.

Fare rules decide whether a fare is refundable, partly refundable or non-refundable. Taxes and carrier surcharges may be refundable even when the fare is not. Where a refund is approved, fees may be deducted according to the fare conditions. All refund rules in this prototype are samples and, in the live system, are configurable and subject to the selected airline’s actual terms.
