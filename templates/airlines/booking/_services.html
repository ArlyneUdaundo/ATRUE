      Coverage & Exclusions, Terms, Conditions & Claim Requirements

      Covered events: Emergency medical expenses, trip cancellation for covered reasons, trip interruption, loss of checked baggage (where applicable).

      Coverage limits: Vary by plan - Basic (up to ₱500,000 medical), Standard (up to ₱1,500,000), Premium (up to ₱3,000,000).

      Exclusions: Pre-existing medical conditions (unless declared and accepted), cancellation due to change of mind, adventure sports not listed, travel against medical/government advice.

      Required supporting documents: Medical certificate (for medical emergencies), proof of event, booking reference, receipts.

      Claim submission instructions: Submit claim with supporting documents within the time limit specified in policy. Claims assessed separately from airline refunds.

      Insurance provider details & applicable terms: Insurance is provided for this prototype only as a sample. A real policy is issued by the insurer, states its own coverage limits, exclusions and claim requirements. Airline refunds and insurance claims are handled separately.
