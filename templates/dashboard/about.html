About ATRUE

Travel and tourism platform for learning, booking, and simulation.

About

ATRUE is a travel and tourism platform developed for educational and practical use. It integrates hotels, resorts, staycations, restaurants, landmarks, activities, tour packages, flights, and land transportation in one system.

The platform supports role-based simulations and real booking workflows for training and operations.
