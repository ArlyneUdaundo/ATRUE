Why ATRUE

Real-world simulation for real careers

ATRUE (Automated Travel, Reservation & Unified Ecosystem) is built exclusively for Saint Joseph College of Baggao to give students hands-on experience with the actual workflows used across the travel and hospitality industry.

9+ Industry Roles

From guests and passengers to tour managers and billing officers.

14 Integrated Modules
